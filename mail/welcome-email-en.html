Ateneo Abierto is a Venezuelan civic learning initiative. Venezuela's education system has been damaged for years — budget cuts, teacher migration, institutions that couldn't keep up. This is the response being built outside that system: hands-on workshops in AI and digital tools, small groups, practical skills people can take and use beyond the room.

No technical background needed.
The entry condition is willingness.

 

What to expect

We're early. The first pilots are being prepared. When there's something real to share — a workshop opening, a new resource, a way to participate — you'll hear from us. We won't send filler.
